## 🧭 Overview

This folder represents the **Business Intelligence layer** of the *Decision-Intelligence-Portfolio*. It showcases how raw business data is converted into **structured information**, **performance monitoring systems**, **risk and compliance visibility**, and **decision-support outputs** for organizational use.

Each project in this folder targets a different organizational function — performance, risk, bidding, competitive positioning, or compliance — collectively demonstrating the range of ways Business Intelligence supports management decision-making.

> **Business Intelligence is not just dashboards.** Dashboards are one delivery mechanism among several. The underlying purpose is a progression:
>
> **Data → Business Information → Performance Visibility → Intelligence → Decision Support**

---

## 🏗️ How This Folder Fits the Portfolio

The repository is organized into four layers, each building toward higher-level organizational decision-making:

| Layer | Folder | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-Data-Analytics` | Analytical data processing and exploration |
| **2** | **`02-Business-Intelligence`** | **Business information, performance monitoring, risk & compliance visibility, decision support** |
| 3 | `03-AI-Automation` | Intelligent automation and applied AI systems |
| 4 | `04-Business-Strategy` | Strategic frameworks and organizational direction |

The Business Intelligence layer sits between raw analytical processing and higher-level strategic decision-making — it is where data becomes **usable, monitored, and actionable business information**.

*(See each folder's own README for details on that layer.)*

## 📐 Business Intelligence Framework

Each project maps to one or more organizational applications of Business Intelligence:

### 🔎 Competitive Intelligence & Strategic Decision Support
Uses structured business and competitive information to support strategic analysis and organizational decision-making.

### 🎯 KPI Performance Analytics
A performance-monitoring and KPI analytics project demonstrating how organizational metrics can be structured and presented to support performance evaluation and management decisions.

### 🏢 Project Bidding Dashboard — Horizon Bid Room
A project-bidding and bid-intelligence solution built around the Horizon Bid Room context. Demonstrates how bidding and project information can be organized and analyzed to support **bid visibility**, **competitive understanding**, and **project evaluation**.

### ⚠️ Risk Analytics Excel Dashboard
A risk-analytics solution providing structured visibility into organizational risks to support risk-based decision-making.

### 📋 Ebra ECC Compliance Dashboard *(upcoming)*
A compliance-monitoring dashboard related to Ebra ECC compliance, to be added to this folder shortly.
